SMS Consent & Messaging Disclosure

This page explains how SMS messages are used for this website experience and serves as proof of consent (opt-in).

How you opt in

By providing your mobile phone number on this website and submitting the form (and/or checking the consent box), you agree to receive SMS messages related to this experience.

Message purpose

  • Account and access-related notifications (e.g., verification / authorization)
  • Security alerts (e.g., unusual activity, access issues)
  • Scavenger hunt status notifications (e.g., clue unlocked, completion confirmation)
  • Optional reminders relevant to the experience

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on activity during the experience. Messages are limited and not sent as marketing.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Opt-out

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP. After opting out, you will no longer receive SMS messages. For help, reply HELP.

Privacy

Your phone number is used only for this website experience and is not sold or shared for advertising.
